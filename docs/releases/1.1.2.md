This release fixes an unresponsive macOS update dialog and makes the update flow easier to follow.

- **macOS updates:** Settings saves and closes before the native update window opens. Automatic prompts wait for other dialogs to close. Release notes are formatted, and **Settings → Updates** also offers a direct link to the official downloads.
- **Dock icon:** IliasSync keeps its dark artwork with the same visual footprint as macOS system icons.
- **Battery power:** The sync card shows the next scheduled check. If the last scheduled sync was skipped on battery, that reason appears in the schedule details.

**If your current Mac update dialog is stuck:** Quit IliasSync and reopen it. Choose **Check for app updates** directly from the IliasSync menu. Alternatively, download the DMG for your Mac and replace IliasSync in Applications. Your materials, settings and sync schedule stay in place.

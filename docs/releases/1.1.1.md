IliasSync 1.1.1 makes it easier to see what a sync is doing and what to do when it needs your attention.

- **A clearer sync area.** The bottom card groups the current status, last run and next scheduled check. During a sync, the current step sits above the progress bar, with pause and stop kept together on the right.
- **A simpler sync icon.** The same circular arrow marks **Sync now** and spins while IliasSync is working.
- **Clearer sign-in recovery.** When ILIAS needs a new sign-in, **Renew sign-in** becomes the main action. The card explains that existing materials remain available while new downloads wait.

**Updating from 1.1.0:** Use **Check for app updates** in IliasSync, or let the app check automatically if you enabled it. Your downloaded materials, university settings and sync schedule stay in place.

IliasSync 1.1.3 makes the workspace easier to navigate and the right installer easier to find.

- **Sidebar:** The sidebar opens and closes smoothly and remembers your width. On Mac, its toggle sits beside the window controls. On Windows and Linux it sits at the top left of the workspace.
- **Find and open files:** Right-click a file in Overview or Materials to show it in Finder or open its containing folder. Clicking away from search hides the caret and keeps your query and results.
- **A quieter workspace:** macOS has a more translucent title bar, with subtler transparency across the content. The sync arrow has more room, and button hover states fade smoothly.
- **Sync details stay put:** Refreshing the library no longer jumps away from the report tab or historical attempt you are reading.
- **Simpler downloads:** The platform installers are linked first. Portable downloads and verification files are grouped below them. Existing automatic update checks keep working with the same signed feeds.
